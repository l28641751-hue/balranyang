Little kittens dive into the sea to eat a colorful pixel-fish picture, one bite at a time!

Balranyang is a cozy puzzle for everyone. Tap a kitten with a colored number and send it to a seat. Each kitten eats only the exposed fish pieces that match its fur color. Plan your order so no seat gets blocked!

• 50 stages that slowly add more colors and seats
• Your own local Top-10 score and rank after each stage
• 10 adorable kittens with pearl foreheads and tiny fish-tail fins
• Earn coins to dress them up with hats, clothes, cushions and toys
• A playful opening where the kittens put on goggles and dive into the sea
• Easy controls for ages 5 to 80
• No ads, no sign-up, no personal data collected. Plays offline.
